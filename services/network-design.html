Network Design & Cabling

Structured cabling, switching, Wi-Fi and multi-site VPN, specified to survive your next three years of headcount.

Delhi NCR onsite coverage 2 hr standard SLA 24/7 monitoring Since 2017

Overview

Networks fail slowly. A office that grew from twelve people to forty on the same consumer router does not break — it just gets worse every month until everyone assumes slow is normal. We survey what is actually there, then design for where the business is going rather than where it was.

What is covered

Structured cabling and patching
Managed switching and VLAN segmentation
Wi-Fi site survey, design and installation
Multi-site and remote-worker VPN
ISP failover and link redundancy
Network documentation and diagrams

Our approach

A physical survey and a wireless heat-map come before any quote, because guessing access-point placement is how offices end up with dead corners. Segmentation is designed in from the start — guests, CCTV and payment devices do not belong on the same flat network as your file server.

Who this is for

Businesses fitting out a new office, outgrowing consumer-grade equipment, or opening a second site that needs to reach the first.

What you get

A documented, segmented network with a labelled patch panel, a Wi-Fi design backed by a survey, and diagrams your next engineer can actually read.
